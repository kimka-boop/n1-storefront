## synthesis (candidate FW26 trend statements)

1. **Vintage-washed, low-saturation treatments define FW26 tops and outer** — thisisneverthat's washed denim shirts and washed polos (burgundy/dark brown/indigo), Covernat's OG chair coat explicitly "lowered saturation + washing effects," Glowny's damage-wash cinch jeans. Supporting records: 4. **Confidence: HIGH**

2. **Loose/relaxed silhouettes beat slim for bottoms and layering** — TNT "relaxed and loose-fit jeans," "effortless layering through relaxed silhouettes"; Covernat WOMAN "relaxed silhouettes"; COS "tailoring loosened through relaxed fits." Records: 3+. **Confidence: HIGH**

3. **Technical/transitional outerwear is the season's anchor purchase** — TNT PERTEX windbreakers + VIK + SKOGAR down, Uniqlo PUFFTECH/Harrington/Light Down Volume/reversible down, Mahagrid raincoat + military coat. Records: 3 brands. **Confidence: HIGH**

4. **Autumn-amped palette: warm brown family (brown/burgundy/indigo) with saturation accents** — Uniqlo names warm brown + vibrant red/energetic blue; TNT burgundy/dark brown/indigo/dusty navy; COS black/white + purple. Records: 3 brands. **Confidence: HIGH**

5. **Knit-first tops programs: funnel necks, V-necks, merino/cashmere fine knits** — COS cashmere funnel-neck jumper + merino crew/cardigan; Glowny Mellow V-Neck Stripe Knit; Uniqlo Souffle Yarn + merino turtleneck/cable. Records: 3 brands. **Confidence: HIGH**

6. **Military/work coats as the winter statement outer** — Mahagrid winter military coat ("LAKE CHILD"), Covernat OG chair coat, Uniqlo Harrington/blouson. Records: 3. **Confidence: HIGH**

7. **Denim runs on two poles: loose washed (men) vs detailed/wide (women)** — TNT loose-fit washed jeans vs Uniqlo Slim Straight + High-Waist Wide Leg + Glowny cinch-back roll-up damage jeans. Records: 3 brands. **Confidence: MEDIUM** (directions differ by gender)

8. **Layering as explicit styling education, incl. engineered "built-in" layers** — Uniqlo HEATTECH + fleece + light jacket formula, TNT layered wardrobe/street utility, Andersson Bell FW26 trompe l'oeil layered tattoo top, Glowny Roan layered long sleeve. Records: 4. **Confidence: HIGH**

9. **Graphic/character IP and novelty prints remain a traffic driver** — TNT graphic tees/fleece, Mahagrid Aurora Borealis prints, Covernat SANA/SOOBIN celebrity lines, Andersson Bell tattoo-print trompe l'oeil. Records: 4. **Confidence: MEDIUM** (novelty/print side has less item-level confirmation)

10. **Women's fitted-romantic counterpoint (corset, V-neck, balloon/stirrup) to oversized menswear** — Glowny corset top/V-neck 3/4 sleeves/wrap skort, COS balloon tailored trousers + stirrup leggings. Records: 2. **Confidence: LOW-MEDIUM** (only 2 brands confirm; Matin Kim FW26 item data unavailable)
